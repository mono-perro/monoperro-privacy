Privacy Policy for Simple Slide

Developer: MonoPerro Studio

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Overview

Simple Slide is a photo slideshow application that allows users to select photos from their device and display them as a slideshow.

Photo Access and Processing

Simple Slide uses the Android system photo picker to let users choose photos. Only the photos selected by the user are accessed for slideshow playback. Photos are processed locally on the device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Information Collection and Sharing

Simple Slide does not require account registration. The app does not collect personal information, transmit selected photos to the developer, or sell or share user data with third parties.

Advertising and Analytics

Simple Slide does not contain advertisements or developer-integrated analytics or tracking services.

Data Storage and Retention

The app does not maintain a remote database of user information or photos. Selected photos remain on the user's device and are not stored on MonoPerro Studio servers.

Children's Privacy

Simple Slide is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Photos selected for slideshow playback are handled locally through Android's photo access mechanisms. No photos are transmitted to MonoPerro Studio.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact MonoPerro Studio at monoperrostudio@gmail.com.
